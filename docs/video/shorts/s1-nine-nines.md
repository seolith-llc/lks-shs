# Short S1 · The nine-nines myth

## The most-quoted number in reliability
- "99.9999999% availability"
- the Erlang AXD301 switch
- quoted everywhere for 20 years

The most quoted reliability number in software is nine nines. The Erlang telephone switch that ran on ninety nine point nine nine nine nine nine nine nine percent availability. You have heard it. It is in every Erlang presentation ever given.

## The source? A PowerPoint slide
-! 99.9999999% | availability claim — from an undocumented slide

Here is the fun part. Joe Armstrong, the creator of Erlang, wrote in his own PhD thesis that the number came from a PowerPoint presentation, and I quote, though how these figures had been obtained was not documented.

## The lesson
- healing claims are cheap
- evidence is precious
- ask HOW it was measured

The lesson is not that Erlang is bad. It is that reliability numbers are folklore until you see the measurement. Full story, with sources, at the repo on screen. Follow for more self-healing software.
